## Full Description

### Pride — Smart Tailor Management System

Pride is a complete tailor shop management application designed especially for Afghan tailors and modern clothing businesses. The app helps tailor shops manage customers, measurements, orders, invoices, reports, finances, and shop operations professionally and efficiently.

Built for Android, iOS, and Web platforms, Pride provides a fast, modern, and reliable experience with both online and offline functionality.

#### Customer Management

* Add and manage customer records
* Save complete customer details
* View customer order history

#### Order Management

* Create tailoring orders easily
* Track order status
* Receive notifications for new, processing, and completed orders

#### Measurement System

* Save detailed body measurements
* Custom fields for tailoring needs
* Support for traditional and modern clothing styles

#### Invoice & Receipt Printing

* Print receipts using thermal printers
* Generate professional PDF invoices
* Share invoices directly with customers

#### Design Catalog

* Add clothing designs and styles
* Share designs with other users
* Organize tailoring patterns and fashion references

#### Finance & Reports

* Daily and monthly financial reports
* Shop performance tracking
* Income and order summaries

#### Shop Dashboard

* View complete shop statistics
* Monitor orders and finances in one place

#### Settings & Customization

* Shop profile management
* Thermal printer configuration
* Notification settings
* Dark mode, light mode, and system theme support

### Works Online & Offline

Pride is designed to work smoothly both online and offline, helping tailor shops continue working without interruptions.

### Built for Modern Tailors

Whether you own a small tailoring shop or manage a large clothing business, Pride helps simplify operations and improve productivity.

# Important Google Play Policy Advice

This is VERY important for your activation-code system.

Since your app is:

* FREE on Play Store
* Uses external activation/payment system

You must be careful.

---

# What NOT To Do

Inside the app:

* Do NOT directly tell users:

  * “Pay via WhatsApp”
  * “Use HesabPay here”
  * “Contact us to buy subscription”

Google may reject it because digital subscriptions normally require Google Play Billing.

---

# Better Safe Method

Inside the app:

* Simply have:

  * “Enter Activation Code”

Without explaining external payment methods.

Then:

* Outside the app
* Via WhatsApp/Facebook/website
* You provide codes manually.

This is much safer for Play Store approval.

---

# Recommended Permissions

Only request permissions you actually need.

Probably:

* Internet
* Bluetooth (thermal printer)
* Notifications
* Storage/files (if needed for PDFs)

Avoid unnecessary permissions.
