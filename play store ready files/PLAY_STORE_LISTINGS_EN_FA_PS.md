### Full description

```
Khayat — Smart Tailor Management System

Khayat is a complete tailor shop management app built for Afghan tailors and modern clothing businesses. Manage customers, measurements, orders, invoices, finances, and daily shop work in one fast, reliable app.

Works on Android, iOS, and Web with online and offline support — your shop keeps running even when the internet is slow or unavailable.

MAIN FEATURES

Customer management
• Add and manage customer records
• Save phone numbers and measurement profiles
• View each customer’s order history

Order management
• Create and track tailoring orders
• Update status, payments, and delivery dates
• Notifications for new and updated orders

Measurements
• Save detailed body measurements
• Custom fields for traditional and modern styles
• Reuse profiles for faster repeat orders

Invoices & printing
• Print receipts on thermal printers (Bluetooth)
• Generate professional PDF invoices
• Share invoices with customers (WhatsApp, etc.)

Design catalog
• Store clothing designs and style references
• Share catalog images with customers
• Organize patterns for Perahan Tonban, waistcoats, and more

Finance & reports
• Payments ledger and income summaries
• Unpaid and delivered order reports
• Shop performance at a glance

Dashboard & settings
• Shop profile and branding
• Thermal printer setup
• Dark mode, light mode, and system theme
• Multi-user shop accounts (owner and staff)

Built for Afghan tailoring
Whether you run a small neighborhood shop or a busy tailoring business, Khayat helps you work faster with RTL support for Dari and Pashto, plus English when you need it.

Free to download. Optional subscription activation keeps advanced sync and licensing features available for your shop.

Support: afzalakbari2017@gmail.com
```
