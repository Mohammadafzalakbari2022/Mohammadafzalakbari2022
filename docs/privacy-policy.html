Privacy Policy

Khayat — tailoring shop management

Effective date: 19 May 2026  |  Last updated: 19 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Khayat (“we”, “our”, “the app”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Khayat mobile and web applications (the “Service”). Khayat is designed for tailoring shops to manage customers, orders, measurements, payments, and related business records.

By creating a shop account or using the Service, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Who we are

Khayat is operated by the developer account holder listed on the Google Play store listing for Khayat. For privacy questions, data requests, or account support, contact us at:

Email: afzalakbari2017@gmail.com

You may update this contact address in Play Console to match your public support email.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the Service. Data falls into these categories:

Account and shop information

  • Shop name and shop identifier
  • Username and password (passwords are stored on our servers only as a secure hash, never in plain text)
  • License and subscription status (trial, activation codes, expiry dates)

Business data you enter (stored on your device and, when online, synced to our servers for your shop)

  • Customer names, phone numbers, addresses, and notes
  • Orders, measurements, design choices, delivery dates, and order status
  • Payment amounts and payment history (ledger entries)
  • Catalog images and design metadata you add
  • Shop logo, shop profile details, and app settings you configure
  • Tasks, notifications, and measurement templates

This business data belongs to your shop. We process it to provide backup, sync, and multi-user access within the same shop. We do not use customer phone numbers for marketing.

Device and optional diagnostics

  • Device type, operating system, and app version may be included in error reports if crash reporting is enabled
  • If you enable push notifications, a device push token may be stored to deliver shop notifications
  • Optional crash reporting (e.g. Sentry) may collect stack traces and technical logs — not your customer lists — when configured in a given build

Permissions you may grant on Android

  • Internet — sign-in, license checks, and data sync
  • Camera / photos — only when you choose to add catalog images or a shop logo from the camera or gallery
  • Contacts — only when you choose to pick a contact to share an invoice or order summary (we do not upload your full address book)

3. How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service (offline use, online sync, printing, sharing)
  • Authenticate users and enforce shop user limits and license rules
  • Process activation codes and subscription-related requests
  • Respond to support requests and password-reset workflows initiated through the app
  • Send push notifications you or shop administrators trigger (where enabled)
  • Monitor reliability and fix bugs (aggregated or technical diagnostics)

We do not sell your personal information or your customers’ data to third parties.

4. Offline storage and sync

Khayat is offline-first. Most business data is stored locally on your device. When you are online and signed in, changes may be sent to our API and stored in our database so other users in the same shop can sync and so your data can be recovered on a new device.

You are responsible for the accuracy and lawfulness of customer data you enter, including obtaining any consent required under local law before storing customers’ phone numbers or photos.

5. Where data is stored and processed

  • On your device — local database and image files in the app’s private storage
  • Our API — hosted on cloud infrastructure (e.g. Render) at pride-v3.onrender.com
  • Database — PostgreSQL hosted with our cloud providers (data encrypted in transit via HTTPS)
  • Web app — static hosting (e.g. Cloudflare Pages) for the browser version of Khayat

Service providers process data only to operate the Service, under contractual and security obligations.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We may share information only in these cases:

  • Within your shop — users you authorize under the same shop account see shared shop data
  • When you share — if you export or share invoices via WhatsApp, SMS, PDF, etc., that sharing is under your control
  • Service providers — hosting, database, optional crash reporting (e.g. Sentry), optional push delivery (e.g. Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Legal — if required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, or security

7. Data retention

We retain account and synced shop data while your shop account is active and as needed to provide the Service. If a shop is disabled or you request deletion, we will delete or anonymize data within a reasonable period, except where we must keep records for legal, security, or billing dispute purposes.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures including HTTPS for network traffic, hashed passwords on the server, and access controls scoped to shop accounts. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; you should protect your device and login credentials.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Access and update most business data directly in the app
  • Request account or data deletion by contacting us at the email above
  • Revoke optional permissions (camera, contacts) in Android system settings
  • Decline push notifications in device settings

Depending on your country, you may have additional rights (access, correction, portability, objection). Contact us to exercise them.

10. Children

The Service is intended for business use by tailoring shops and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Subscriptions and activation codes

Khayat may be listed as a free app on app stores. Paid features may be unlocked with activation codes obtained outside the app (e.g. through support or manual payment instructions). Those transactions are handled according to our support process, not through Google Play billing, unless we explicitly offer in-app purchases in the future.

12. International users

Our servers may be located outside your country. By using the Service, you understand that data may be processed in jurisdictions with different data-protection laws than your own.
